04 — how I work

Define. Decompose. Experiment.

Define the metric.

Start from the number that matters — activation, retention, order frequency — and justify why. A metric without a “why this one” is just a dashboard.

Find the trust gap.

Products optimize operational metrics; users optimize trust. The highest-leverage work sits between the two. I look for that misalignment first.

Design the experiment.

Hypothesis, cohort, guardrails, a numeric success threshold. If it cannot be falsified, it is not a plan.
